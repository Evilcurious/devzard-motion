Commission & Net Profit Guide

How the 70 / 30 split is calculated, verified and paid — explained line by line.

1. The formula

        Gross Client Revenue  (contract value, excluding sales tax)
      − Direct Project Expenses  (agreed, documented, project-specific)
      ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
      = Net Profit
      × 30%  =  Sales Partner Commission
      × 70%  =  Devzard Share

Two things follow from this formula, and they are the two things that matter most:

  1. Your commission moves with profit, not with price. A big project with heavy third-party costs can pay less than a smaller project with light costs.
  2. Every rupee of unnecessary expense costs you 30 paisa. Every rupee of discount you invent costs you 30 paisa. Never spend money on a project, and never reduce a price, without written approval.

4. The four expense rules

Rule 1 — Project-specific. If the cost would exist without this project, it is not deducted.

Rule 2 — Documented. No receipt, no deduction. You may ask to see the receipts for any project.

Rule 3 — The Big Expense Rule. Any single project expense above PKR 25,000, or any expense that changes the price of the project, must be approved by you in writing (WhatsApp counts) before it is committed. Silence is not approval — we will ask, and we will wait.

Rule 4 — Shared costs are apportioned fairly. A tool used across three clients is charged one third to each. Apportionment is shown on the statement.

7. Payout timeline

Client pays Devzard ──► Payment clears ──► Milestone delivered ──► Statement issued ──► Payout within 7 business days
  • Commission on an invoice is payable only once that invoice is received and cleared.
  • Multi-milestone projects are paid milestone by milestone.
  • Payouts are made by bank transfer (IBFT / Raast) to the account named in your agreement, on the last day of a payout run, or sooner.
  • Minimum payout: PKR 5,000. Smaller balances carry forward to the next payout.
  • A short statement accompanies every payment: project, client revenue, expenses, net profit and your share.

8. Verification and disputes

You should never have to take our word for a number.

  1. Ask for the working. Any project, any time. You get the revenue figure, each expense line, and the resulting split.
  2. Ask for receipts. Available for every deducted expense without argument.
  3. Raise a query within 15 days of receiving a statement. Disputed amounts are held back and paid once resolved — the undisputed balance is paid on time regardless.
  4. Escalate. Unresolved after 15 more days? It goes to the founders, in writing, and the partnership agreement's dispute clause applies.
