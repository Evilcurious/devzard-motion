Sales Partnership Agreement

THIS AGREEMENT is made on [DATE] between:

(1) Devzard — [FULL LEGAL NAME OF BUSINESS], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], Pakistan, contact: [NAME], [EMAIL], [PHONE] ("Devzard", "we", "us"); and

(2) [PARTNER FULL NAME] — CNIC / ID No. [NUMBER], address [ADDRESS], contact: [EMAIL], [PHONE], bank account: [BANK, TITLE, IBAN] ("Sales Partner", "you").

Devzard and the Sales Partner are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

Recitals

  • A. Devzard designs and develops websites, e-commerce stores, custom software and digital solutions for clients.
  • B. The Sales Partner has contacts, networks and market knowledge that can generate client opportunities for Devzard.
  • C. The Parties wish to work together on the terms set out below, under which the Sales Partner is rewarded with a share of the net profit of each successful project.

IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS.

1. Definitions

1.1 In this Agreement:

  • "Client" means a person or business that Devzard, introduced by the Sales Partner, enters into a contract with for Services.
  • "Client Revenue" means the gross amount invoiced to and payable by the Client for a Project, excluding sales tax and other taxes charged on the invoice.
  • "Commission" means 30% of Net Profit, calculated under clause 7.
  • "Direct Expenses" means the project-specific, documented third-party costs defined in clause 7.3, and nothing else.
  • "Excluded Entity" means any business listed in Schedule D, or any business that Devzard has already contracted with or has an active written proposal or negotiation with at the date a lead is registered.
  • "Lead" means a prospective Client identified by the Sales Partner.
  • "Net Profit" means Client Revenue received, less Direct Expenses, as described in clause 7.
  • "Project" means the delivery of Services to a Client under a contract between Devzard and that Client.
  • "Qualified Lead" means a Lead that has (a) a named decision-maker, (b) a genuine requirement that Devzard's Services can meet, and (c) agreed to a discovery conversation.
  • "Registered Lead" means a Lead recorded in the Lead Register and accepted by Devzard under clause 4.
  • "Service" or "Services" means the design, development, marketing and digital services offered by Devzard from time to time, including those listed in Schedule A.
  • "Lead Register" means the shared tracker identified in clause 4.1 (currently [PLATFORM / FILE NAME]).
  • "Trial Period" means the initial 60-day period described in clause 11.

1.2 Headings are for convenience only. References to a clause include its sub-clauses. The singular includes the plural.

2. Appointment

2.1 Devzard appoints the Sales Partner as a non-exclusive sales partner for its Services, and the Sales Partner accepts that appointment, on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Sales Partner's role is to identify and approach potential Clients, introduce them to Devzard, and support the opportunity to the point of a signed contract and payment. The Sales Partner is not required to perform, and must not attempt to perform, any technical, design or development work.

2.3 Non-exclusive. Neither Party is restricted from working with any other person or business. The Sales Partner may represent other agencies, hold other jobs, and run their own business.

2.4 The Sales Partner has no authority to negotiate, vary, sign, or terminate any contract with a Client, to quote or agree a price, or to make any commitment on Devzard's behalf. All contracts with Clients are between Devzard and the Client.

3. Sales Partner obligations

3.1 The Sales Partner will:

  • (a) identify and approach potential Clients through their own network, social media, community and business contacts;
  • (b) describe Devzard's Services accurately and only in the terms provided in the current service catalogue and pitch materials;
  • (c) register each Lead in the Lead Register before contacting it (clause 4);
  • (d) gather the basic qualifying information for each Lead and record it in the Lead Register;
  • (e) keep in touch with the Client after the contract is signed, without interfering with Devzard's delivery of the Services;
  • (f) respond to Client and Devzard communications within 24 hours on business days;
  • (g) keep Devzard informed of the status of every Lead and opportunity at least weekly, and immediately on any material development;
  • (h) comply with all applicable laws, and with reasonable instructions from Devzard regarding brand, tone and messaging.

3.2 The Sales Partner will not:

  • (a) quote, promise, estimate or discuss a price, discount, payment schedule or delivery date without prior written approval from Devzard;
  • (b) offer any discount, rebate, gift or inducement to a Client, or to any employee or representative of a Client, without prior written approval from Devzard;
  • (c) make any statement that Devzard is able to deliver something that has not been confirmed in writing by Devzard;
  • (d) represent themselves as an employee, officer, agent or legal representative of Devzard, or use Devzard's name or marks except as permitted under clause 17.5;
  • (e) approach any Excluded Entity;
  • (f) send unsolicited bulk or "spam" communications, or contact anyone who has asked not to be contacted;
  • (g) collect, request or receive any payment from a Client, or accept money on Devzard's behalf;
  • (h) make any commitment regarding the ownership, licensing or hosting of a Client's data or website beyond what is stated in Devzard's standard client terms.

4. Lead registration and attribution

4.1 Devzard will maintain a shared Lead Register at [PLATFORM / FILE NAME], accessible to the Sales Partner, in which Leads are recorded with: date, business name, contact name and designation, contact details, source, industry, requirement summary and estimated value.

4.2 The Sales Partner must register a Lead before contacting it. Devzard will respond to a registration (accepting or objecting) within 2 business days. If Devzard does not respond within that period, the Lead is deemed accepted.

4.3 A Registered Lead is protected for 30 days from the date of registration. The protection period is renewed and extended automatically while there is documented activity — a held discovery call, a proposal issued, or an active negotiation.

4.4 Devzard may decline to accept a Lead only where the Lead is an Excluded Entity, where the Lead has asked Devzard not to be contacted, or where the Lead is already registered by another sales partner. A decline must state the reason.

4.5 If two or more partners register the same Lead, the earliest valid registration has priority. Where both have genuinely contributed to the opportunity, clause 10.2 applies.

4.6 Commission is payable on a Project only where the Lead was a Registered Lead and the Client contract was signed within 12 months of registration (or within any extended protection period agreed in writing).

5. Devzard obligations

5.1 Devzard will:

  • (a) supply the Sales Partner with a current service catalogue, pricing guidance, pitch materials, portfolio links and brand assets;
  • (b) provide a named point of contact and respond to the Sales Partner's questions about a Lead within one business day;
  • (c) join discovery calls, qualify requirements, and prepare and issue proposals and quotations;
  • (d) keep the Lead Register and the opportunity status up to date and visible to the Sales Partner;
  • (e) record, document and disclose Direct Expenses as required by clause 7, and obtain approval for those above the threshold in clause 7.5;
  • (f) calculate Commission transparently and issue a written statement with every payment;
  • (g) use reasonable commercial efforts to deliver the Services professionally, so that commissions are actually earned;
  • (h) not deliberately divert a Registered Lead's opportunity into a structure intended to avoid Commission, including by splitting the contract into separate documents.

6. Commission

6.1 For every successful Project, the Net Profit will be divided:

PartyShare of Net Profit
Devzard70%
Sales Partner30%

6.2 Worked example. A Project generating PKR 15,000 of Net Profit pays Devzard PKR 10,500 and the Sales Partner PKR 4,500. This is illustrative only. Actual profits vary with the Project's price, scope and Direct Expenses, and the Sales Partner's earnings depend entirely on the Projects they successfully introduce.

6.3 No Commission is earned, and none is payable, until: (a) the Client has paid Devzard the relevant amount, and it has cleared; and (b) the related milestone has been delivered.

6.4 Commission is the Sales Partner's entire remuneration under this Agreement. No salary, retainer, fee, expense reimbursement or benefit of any kind is payable.

7. Calculation of Net Profit

7.1 Formula.

Net Profit = Client Revenue received − Direct Expenses
Commission = Net Profit × 30%

7.2 Client Revenue is stated exclusive of sales tax and any other tax charged on the Client invoice.

7.3 Direct Expenses are limited to the following, where they relate specifically to that Project and are supported by an invoice or receipt:

  • (a) domain names, hosting, SSL certificates and cloud infrastructure for the Project;
  • (b) premium themes, plugins, software licences and fonts purchased for the Project;
  • (c) stock images, video, audio and design assets licensed for the Project;
  • (d) paid APIs, integrations, SMS, payment gateway setup and AI/service credits consumed by the Project;
  • (e) subcontracted or outsourced delivery — freelancers, designers, copywriters, QA, photography;
  • (f) payment gateway, bank and transfer fees actually incurred in collecting the Client's money;
  • (g) client-specific advertising spend that the Client has not separately reimbursed;
  • (h) hardware, printing, courier and similar physical costs bought specifically for the Project;
  • (i) taxes, duties or levies payable by Devzard that arise directly and solely from the Project (excluding income tax on Devzard's own profits).

7.4 Never deducted as Direct Expenses: Devzard's salaries, contractor pay for its own core team, office and premises costs, utilities, internet, internal software subscriptions, general marketing and advertising, business development costs, accounting and legal fees, rework or remediation arising from Devzard's own errors or omissions, and any penalty, credit or service-level payment made to a Client because Devzard delivered late. Where such a penalty has been applied to Client Revenue, it is added back before calculating Net Profit.

7.5 Approval threshold. Any single Direct Expense above PKR 25,000, and any expense or variation that would change the price payable by the Client, requires the Sales Partner's prior written approval, which may be given by WhatsApp or email. Where approval is not obtained, that expense is not deducted in calculating Net Profit.

7.6 Shared costs used for two or more clients are apportioned between them on a reasonable and consistent basis, and the apportionment is disclosed on the Commission statement.

7.7 Discounts. Any discount, price reduction or free addition to a Project requires Devzard's prior written approval, and reduces Client Revenue accordingly. Unapproved discounts are treated as a reduction of Client Revenue, and therefore reduce Net Profit and Commission.

7.8 Change requests. Additional work agreed with the Client after the contract is signed is quoted and invoiced separately and is treated as added Client Revenue, generating additional Commission on the same 70/30 basis.

7.9 Verification. At the Sales Partner's request, Devzard will provide the underlying calculation and, on reasonable request, copies of the invoices or receipts supporting any deducted Direct Expense.

8. Payment of Commission

8.1 Commission is payable within 7 business days after both conditions in clause 6.3 are satisfied, by bank transfer to the account named in this Agreement.

8.2 On multi-milestone or installment Projects, Commission is calculated and paid per installment received, against the Direct Expenses incurred up to that point, so that by final completion the totals equal 30% of total Net Profit.

8.3 Minimum payout. Balances below PKR 5,000 are carried forward and paid together with the next payout or at termination, whichever is earlier.

8.4 Statements. Every payment is accompanied by a written statement showing: Client and Project, Client Revenue received, each Direct Expense line, Net Profit, the split, and the amount paid.

8.5 Queries. The Sales Partner may raise a query on any statement within 15 days. Disputed amounts are held back pending resolution; undisputed amounts are paid on time.

8.6 Tax. Where Devzard is required by law to withhold tax from a payment to the Sales Partner, it will do so, remit it to the authority, and provide the Sales Partner with a withholding certificate.

9. Recurring revenue, renewals and upsells

9.1 The Sales Partner earns 30% of Net Profit on recurring and repeat revenue from a Registered Lead's Client, including maintenance and support retainers, hosting, SEO and marketing retainers, licence renewals, and follow-on or additional projects, for as long as this Agreement is in force.

9.2 Recurring Commission is calculated monthly (or per billing cycle) on amounts actually received in that period, less Direct Expenses for that period, and is paid under clause 8.

9.3 Commission on upsells, add-ons and change requests introduced by Devzard's delivery team (rather than by the Sales Partner) is payable at 15% of Net Profit. Where the Sales Partner introduces or supports the additional work, the full 30% applies.

Optional variant — declining renewal rate. If you prefer a declining rate on long-running accounts, replace clause 9.1 with: 30% of Net Profit on recurring revenue for the first 12 months of the Client relationship, and 15% thereafter for as long as this Agreement is in force. Delete this box if you keep the standard terms.

10. Multiple partners and blended attribution

10.1 Where the same Lead is validly worked by more than one partner, Devzard will inform both partners promptly and in writing.

10.2 The partners may agree a split of the 30% between them, and will confirm it to Devzard in writing. If they cannot agree within 10 business days, the 30% is divided proportionally to each partner's documented contribution as assessed in good faith by Devzard, with input from both partners, and the assessment and its reasons are shared with both.

10.3 Devzard will not pay a total Commission exceeding 30% of Net Profit on a single Project, except under the optional bonus in the Program Overview if it has been expressly adopted in writing.

11. Trial Period

11.1 The first 60 days from the date of this Agreement are a Trial Period.

11.2 The default Trial Period targets are: 20 Qualified Leads registered, 6 discovery calls held, 4 proposals issued, and 1 Project closed. The Parties may record different targets in Schedule E before the Trial Period begins.

11.3 Commission is payable on Projects closed during the Trial Period on exactly the same terms as afterwards.

11.4 At the end of the Trial Period the Parties will review performance and agree in writing to one of: (a) continue this Agreement without a fixed end date; (b) extend the Trial Period for up to 30 further days; (c) change the target segment, channel or messaging and continue; or (d) terminate.

11.5 If neither Party gives notice under clause 11.4, this Agreement continues as an open-ended partnership on the same terms.

12. Non-payment, refunds and adjustments

12.1 No Commission is earned on any amount the Client has not paid. Where a Client pays in part, Commission is calculated on the part received.

12.2 Where a Client's payment is refunded, reversed, charged back, or recovered by Devzard as a bad debt, any Commission paid on that amount is recoverable and may be set off against future Commission. If no future Commission arises within 6 months, the amount is repayable on demand.

12.3 Where a Client withholds or delays payment in a genuine dispute about the Services, Commission on the disputed amount is paid only when and to the extent that Devzard receives it. Commission on undisputed amounts is paid on time.

12.4 Where a refund or credit arises from the Sales Partner's material misrepresentation or breach of this Agreement, the resulting loss is borne by the Sales Partner and set off against their Commission.

12.5 The Sales Partner is never liable to Devzard for a Project that makes a loss. No negative balance arises from clause 7, except through clauses 12.2 and 12.4.

13. Confidentiality

13.1 Each Party will keep confidential all non-public information received from the other, including Client identities and contact details, pricing, cost structures, margins, proposals, delivery methods, the Lead Register, and the terms of this Agreement.

13.2 Confidential information may be used only to perform this Agreement. It may not be disclosed to any third party without prior written consent, except where required by law.

13.3 These obligations continue for 3 years after termination, and indefinitely in respect of Client personal data and trade secrets.

13.4 The Sales Partner will handle Client personal data lawfully, share it with Devzard only for the purpose of the opportunity, and delete it on request once the opportunity has ended.

14. Intellectual property

14.1 All intellectual property created in delivering the Services — code, designs, content, documentation and systems — belongs to Devzard or to the Client as set out in Devzard's contract with the Client. The Sales Partner acquires no rights in it.

14.2 The Sales Partner acquires no rights in Devzard's name, logo, marks, templates, pitch materials or portfolio.

14.3 The Sales Partner will not reuse, resell or replicate any Client deliverable, Devzard proposal, quotation or internal document.

15. Non-circumvention

15.1 The Sales Partner will not, during this Agreement and for 12 months after it ends, directly or indirectly solicit, contract with, or supply services substantially similar to the Services to any Registered Lead's Client, other than through Devzard.

15.2 The Sales Partner will not introduce a Registered Lead's Client to a competing supplier for the purpose of displacing Devzard.

15.3 Devzard will not circumvent the Sales Partner: where Devzard contracts with a Registered Lead's Client for Services, Commission is payable under this Agreement even if Devzard signed the contract directly, provided the Lead was registered and the protection conditions in clause 4 are met.

15.4 Clauses 15.1 and 15.2 apply only to Clients that became Clients through the Sales Partner's Registered Leads, and only for the duration stated. They do not restrict the Sales Partner's general business activity.

16. Non-solicitation of personnel

16.1 Neither Party will, during this Agreement and for 12 months after it ends, knowingly solicit for employment any employee or core contractor of the other Party who was involved in the Projects, without the other Party's written consent.

16.2 This clause does not prevent responses to general public advertisements.

17. Compliance and representations

17.1 Each Party represents that it has the capacity and authority to enter into this Agreement, and that entering into it does not breach any other obligation it owes.

17.2 Anti-bribery. The Sales Partner will not offer, promise, give or accept any bribe, kickback, facilitation payment or improper advantage in connection with any Lead, Client or public official. Any breach is a material breach of this Agreement.

17.3 Anti-spam. The Sales Partner will comply with applicable law on unsolicited communications and data protection, will identify themselves honestly in outreach, and will stop contacting anyone who asks them to.

17.4 Accurate claims. The Sales Partner will not overstate Devzard's experience, capacity, client list or results, and will not offer any guarantee of outcome, ranking or revenue.

17.5 Brand use. The Sales Partner may use Devzard's name, logo and approved materials only to promote Devzard's Services, in the form supplied, and must stop on termination.

18. Independent contractor status and taxes

18.1 The Sales Partner is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, agency, partnership or joint venture relationship. The Sales Partner is not entitled to any employment benefit, notice, gratuity, provident fund or leave.

18.2 The Sales Partner is responsible for their own income tax and other statutory obligations arising from Commission, and for registering for a National Tax Number and, where eligible, appearing on the Active Taxpayer List. Devzard may withhold tax from payments where the law requires it (clause 8.6).

18.3 The Sales Partner bears their own costs of doing business — transport, internet, phone, data, office and marketing.

18.4 The Sales Partner is free to determine how, when and where they work, subject to clause 3.

19. Limitation of liability

19.1 Devzard's total liability to the Sales Partner under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to the Commission properly payable for Registered Leads under clauses 6 to 9.

19.2 Neither Party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue, goodwill or opportunity, except as expressly provided in this Agreement.

19.3 Nothing in this clause limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

20. Term and termination

20.1 This Agreement starts on the date above and, after the Trial Period, continues until terminated under this clause.

20.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving 30 days' written notice. During the Trial Period, either Party may terminate by giving 7 days' written notice.

20.3 Either Party may terminate immediately by written notice for fraud, bribery, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality, or any material breach that is not remedied within 14 days of written notice.

20.4 Tail protection. If the Sales Partner's Registered Lead signs a contract with Devzard within 90 days after termination, the Commission remains payable under this Agreement, and is paid when the conditions in clause 6.3 are met.

20.5 On termination: the Sales Partner returns or deletes all confidential information, stops using Devzard's name and marks, and hands over all Lead information and Client contacts. Devzard pays all Commission earned and payable on or before the termination date, together with amounts carried forward under clause 8.3, within 30 days.

20.6 Clauses 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 19, 21 and 20.4 and 20.5 survive termination.

21. Governing law and dispute resolution

21.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan.

21.2 The Parties will first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith discussion within 15 days of written notice of the dispute.

21.3 If it is not resolved, the Parties will refer the matter to mediation in Lahore before a mediator agreed between them, or appointed by a recognised mediation body, sharing the cost equally.

21.4 If mediation fails within 30 days, the dispute is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts at Lahore, Pakistan.

21.5 Nothing in this clause prevents either Party from applying for urgent injunctive relief to protect confidential information.

22. Notices

22.1 Notices under this Agreement may be given by email to the addresses in this Agreement, or by WhatsApp to the numbers recorded in the Lead Register, and are treated as received on the next business day.

22.2 The Parties will keep their contact and bank details current and notify each other of any change within 5 business days.

23. General

23.1 Entire agreement. This Agreement, together with its Schedules, is the entire agreement between the Parties on its subject matter and replaces all prior discussions.

23.2 Amendment. No amendment is effective unless in writing and signed (or confirmed by email) by both Parties.

23.3 No waiver. A failure or delay in enforcing a right is not a waiver of it.

23.4 Assignment. The Sales Partner may not assign this Agreement without Devzard's written consent. Devzard may assign it to a successor in title of its business.

23.5 Severability. If a provision is found unenforceable, the rest of the Agreement continues in force.

23.6 Counterparts and e-signature. This Agreement may be signed in counterparts, and electronic or scanned signatures are valid and binding.
