# Sales Partnership Program — Program Overview

**Devzard — Let's Build Together.**

---

## 1. The opportunity

Devzard builds websites, online stores, custom software and digital systems for businesses. Our delivery team is strong. What we need is **reach** — people who know businesses, who are trusted in their networks, and who can open doors we cannot open on our own.

That is what this program is. You bring the relationships and the conversations. We bring the technology, the proposals and the delivery. When a deal closes and the invoice is paid, we split the profit **70 / 30** in your favour of the sales side.

You do not need to write a single line of code. You need to be good at talking to people and following up.

## 3. What the sales partner does

**Find.** Look for businesses and individuals in your network, on social media, in your city and in your industry who may need a website, an online store, an app or a digital system.

**Reach out.** Start conversations. Ask questions. Understand what the business actually needs and what it is costing them to go without it.

**Introduce.** Bring Devzard into the conversation. Position us clearly: what we do, who we have done it for, how we work.

**Qualify.** Find out the basics — what they want, roughly what budget they have in mind, and when they want to start. Log every lead in the shared tracker.

**Support the close.** Sit in on the discovery call. Help chase the decision. Follow up when we send a proposal. Keep the client warm while the technical team does its job.

**Hand over and stay in touch.** Once the project starts, you are the relationship, not the delivery. Keep the client informed and look for the next project, the referral and the upgrade.

> **What you never do:** quote a price, promise a timeline, sign anything, or claim Devzard can do something we have not confirmed. Get those answers from the team — usually within the same day.

## 6. How you earn — 70 / 30

For every successful project, the **net profit** is divided as follows, after deducting the agreed project expenses:

- **70% — Devzard**
- **30% — Sales Partner**

| Line | Amount (PKR) |

> This is only an example. Actual profits vary with the project's price, scope and expenses. Your earnings depend entirely on the successful deals you bring in — nothing here is a guarantee of income.

Full detail on what counts as an expense, how the split is calculated and how you verify it is in **`03-commission-guide.md`**.

## 7. How we keep it transparent

Three rules protect you from surprise deductions:

1. **Expenses only count if they are project-specific and documented.** Domain, hosting, premium plugins, licences, stock assets, outsourced work, gateway fees — with receipts.
2. **Devzard's internal costs are never deducted.** Salaries, office, tools, subscriptions, general marketing and our own overheads come out of the 70%, not out of your 30%.
3. **You get a written statement for every project.** Client revenue, every expense line, net profit, your share. You have 15 days to raise any query.

## 8. Your first 60 days — the trial period

We start with a short trial to see how outreach performs and how conversions go. It also protects both sides: it lets you test the program without a long commitment.

| Phase | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Day 0** | Agreement signed, added to the lead tracker and WhatsApp group, 1-hour onboarding session, service catalogue and pitch deck handed over |
| **Days 1–7** | You register your first 10 target leads. We review them together and agree who to approach first |
| **Days 8–30** | Outreach begins. Target: 20 qualified leads registered, first 3–5 discovery calls booked |
| **Days 31–60** | Proposals in the market. Target: at least 1 project closed and invoiced. We shadow and support every call |
| **Day 60 review** | Together we look at outreach volume, interest generated and conversions, then decide: **expand**, **extend the trial**, or **part ways** |

**Default trial targets** (adjustable by mutual agreement before you start):

|---|---|

| Discovery calls held | 6 |
| Proposals sent | 4 |

> If the trial succeeds, we move to an open-ended partnership with a monthly pipeline review and no end date.

## 10. What we expect from you

- **Honesty.** Never overstate what we can do, never invent clients or results, never collect money on our behalf.
- **Register before you pitch.** Log the lead before you contact them. This is what protects your 30%.
- **Respect the client list.** Some businesses are already Devzard clients. We will share that list with you.
- **Respond within 24 hours.** Speed closes deals, especially on WhatsApp.
- **Never quote a price or a deadline** without written confirmation from Devzard.
- **No spam.** Targeted outreach, not mass blasts. Our reputation and your network are both on the line.
- **Keep client information confidential.** Share it with Devzard only, and only for the purpose of the deal.
- **Stay compliant.** No kickbacks to employees of client organisations, no bribery, no misrepresentation.

## 11. Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to be a developer?**
No. Not even close. Your job is finding clients, starting conversations and keeping the deal moving. Everything technical is handled by our team.

**Is 30% on the full project price?**
No — 30% of **net profit**, which is what remains after the agreed, documented project expenses are deducted. See the commission guide for exactly what qualifies.

**When exactly do I get paid?**
Within 7 business days of (a) the client's payment being received and cleared by Devzard, and (b) the related milestone being delivered. On multi-milestone projects you are paid milestone by milestone.

**What if the client never pays?**
No commission is payable on money that was never received. If a client pays in part, you are paid on the part received. If a payment is later refunded or reversed, that amount is adjusted against your next payout.

**Is this a job? Is there a salary?**
No. This is an independent contractor partnership, commission-only. You are not an employee, and you must arrange your own tax affairs.

**Can I work with other companies or agencies at the same time?**
Yes — this partnership is non-exclusive. You can keep your job and your other clients too.

**What if I bring a client Devzard already has?**
You will be given a list of existing clients and active leads. Those are excluded. Anything not on that list is new business — register it, and it is protected for you.

**What if a project ends up with no profit?**
Then there is no commission, and you do not owe anything. You are never charged for a loss-making project. What you must not do is give unapproved discounts — those come straight out of net profit.

**Do I earn on recurring revenue too?**
Yes. Maintenance, hosting, SEO and retainers earn you 30% of net profit for as long as the partnership is active and the client stays.

**Can I do this part-time?**
Yes. Most partners start part-time and 5–10 focused hours a week is enough to build a real pipeline.

**Do I have to sign anything?**
Yes — a simple one-document partnership agreement that sets out the 70/30 split, lead protection, payment terms and confidentiality. It protects your commission as much as ours.

**What does it cost me to join?**
Nothing. There is no joining fee, no subscription and no deposit — ever.
