# Partner Onboarding Checklist

*Everything that has to happen between "yes" and "first commission".*

---

## Step 0 — Before you sign

- [ ] Read the **Program Overview** (`01-program-overview.md`) and the **Commission Guide** (`03-commission-guide.md`)
- [ ] Read the **Partnership Agreement** (`02-partner-agreement.md`) end to end, including the Schedules
- [ ] Confirm the two numbers that matter most to you: **30% of net profit** and **payout within 7 business days of payment + delivery**
- [ ] Ask anything you do not understand. Undisclosed confusion becomes a dispute later

---

## Step 1 — Sign and set up (Day 0)

| # | Task | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign the partnership agreement (e-signature or scan is fine) | Partner + Devzard |
| 2 | Exchange contact details, bank account for payouts, CNIC / NTN where available | Partner |
| 3 | Receive the **client exclusion list** — existing Devzard clients and active leads | Devzard |
| 4 | Get added to the **lead tracker** and the partner **WhatsApp group** | Devzard |
| 5 | Receive the **service catalogue and pricing guide** | Devzard |
| 6 | Receive the **pitch deck**, portfolio links and one-pagers (English + Urdu) | Devzard |
| 7 | Book the 1-hour onboarding session | Both |

---

## Step 2 — The onboarding session (Day 0–3, one hour)

**Agenda:**

1. How Devzard works — services, delivery process, what a typical project looks like from the inside (15 min)
2. Pricing, scope and margins — how to size a deal and what is realistic to promise (15 min)
3. The lead tracker — how to register a lead and why attribution discipline protects your 30% (10 min)
4. Pitching — how to introduce Devzard without sounding like an advertisement (15 min)
5. Open floor — your network, your industry, your first 10 targets (5 min)

**You leave with:** the pitch in your own words, the price bands in your head, and 10 named targets.

---

## Step 3 — Week 1 (Days 1–7)

- [ ] List 25 businesses or individuals in your network who could plausibly buy from Devzard
- [ ] Register your **first 10 target leads** in the tracker — company, contact, contact details, source, why you think they need us
- [ ] Review those 10 with the Devzard team; agree who to approach first and in what order
- [ ] Write and rehearse your first-touch message (use the templates in `04-outreach-playbook.md`, then make it your own)
- [ ] Send your first 5 first-touch messages
- [ ] Book your first discovery call

---

## Step 4 — Days 8–30

- [ ] Register 20 qualified leads in total
- [ ] Contact every registered lead at least once
- [ ] Book and hold 3–5 discovery calls (Devzard joins each one)
- [ ] Watch at least one proposal as it is written so you know what is in it
- [ ] Log every conversation and every next step in the tracker
- [ ] Weekly 15-minute check-in with the team for the first month

---

## Step 5 — Days 31–60

- [ ] Keep the pipeline moving — no lead left without a next action and a date
- [ ] Follow up on every proposal at least three times (most deals are won on follow-up three, not call one)
- [ ] Close your first project
- [ ] Receive your first commission statement — check it against the Commission Guide line by line
- [ ] Ask the client for a testimonial and one referral

---

## Day 60 — Trial review

| Metric | Default target | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified leads registered | 20 | |
| Leads contacted | 20 | |
| Discovery calls held | 6 | |
| Proposals sent | 4 | |
| Projects closed | 1 | |
| Commission paid | — | |

**Outcomes:**

- **Expand.** Targets met or close to met → open-ended partnership, monthly review, priority on new leads.
- **Extend.** Strong outreach, deals still in the pipeline → extend the trial by 30 days and re-review.
- **Adjust.** Outreach is working but the target market is wrong → change segment, channel or message and run again.
- **Part ways.** No outreach, no activity, no pipeline → clean exit, no hard feelings, no obligations either side.

---

## Day 1 — what to do right now

1. Open the lead tracker and write down **10 names**. Not companies you have heard of — people who would actually take your call.
2. Pick the two strongest. Send the first-touch WhatsApp template from the outreach playbook.
3. Message the Devzard WhatsApp group with those two names so the team knows who is being approached and can prepare.

That is it. Pipeline first, perfection later.
