# Commission & Net Profit Guide

*How the 70 / 30 split is calculated, verified and paid — explained line by line.*

---

## 1. The formula

```
        Gross Client Revenue  (contract value, excluding sales tax)
      − Direct Project Expenses  (agreed, documented, project-specific)
      ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
      = Net Profit
      × 30%  =  Sales Partner Commission
      × 70%  =  Devzard Share
```

Two things follow from this formula, and they are the two things that matter most:

1. **Your commission moves with profit, not with price.** A big project with heavy third-party costs can pay less than a smaller project with light costs.
2. **Every rupee of unnecessary expense costs you 30 paisa.** Every rupee of discount you invent costs you 30 paisa. Never spend money on a project, and never reduce a price, without written approval.

---

## 2. Step by step

| Step | What happens | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client agrees to the proposal and pays | Devzard signs with the client |
| 2 | Gross client revenue is recorded | Contract value, excluding sales tax |
| 3 | Expenses are logged with receipts as they are incurred | Devzard, with receipts kept on file |
| 4 | Net profit is calculated | Closing revenue − documented direct expenses |
| 5 | Your 30% is calculated and shown on a statement | Commission statement, per project |
| 6 | Statement is shared with you | With every payout |
| 7 | You check it. Query window: 15 days | Raise anything unclear with the team |
| 8 | Payout is sent | Within 7 business days of client payment + milestone delivery |

## 3. What counts as a direct project expense

Only costs that **belong to that specific project** and would not exist without it. Every line needs a receipt or an invoice on file.

### Deductible (reduces the profit pool)

| Expense | Notes |
|---|---|
| Domain name and hosting for the project | Only the period charged to the project |
| Premium themes, plugins and licences | Bought for this client, licensed to this client |
| Stock images, video, fonts and assets | Where a licence fee is paid |
| Paid APIs and third-party services | Payment gateways, SMS, maps, AI credits used by the project |
| Outsourced / subcontracted delivery | Freelance developers, designers, copywriters, QA |
| Client-specific ad spend | Only where the client has not reimbursed it directly |
| Payment gateway and bank transfer fees | The real cost of collecting the client's money |
| Physical costs for the project | Printing, courier, hardware purchased for the project |

### Never deductible (Devzard absorbs these in its 70%)

| Expense | Why |
|---|---|
| Devzard salaries and team time | Our cost of doing business |
| Office rent, utilities, internet | Overhead |
| Software subscriptions and internal tools | Overhead |
| General marketing and advertising of Devzard | Overhead |
| Rework caused by our own mistakes | Our problem, not yours |
| Penalties paid to a client because Devzard delivered late | Added back before your commission is calculated |
| Anything not agreed in advance, above the pre-approval threshold | See rule 4 below |

---

## 4. The four expense rules

> **Rule 1 — Project-specific.** If the cost would exist without this project, it is not deducted.
>
> **Rule 2 — Documented.** No receipt, no deduction. You may ask to see the receipts for any project.
>
> **Rule 3 — The Big Expense Rule.** Any single project expense above **PKR 25,000**, or any expense that changes the price of the project, must be approved by you in writing (WhatsApp counts) before it is committed. Silence is not approval — we will ask, and we will wait.
>
> **Rule 4 — Shared costs are apportioned fairly.** A tool used across three clients is charged one third to each. Apportionment is shown on the statement.

### Example 7 — The unapproved discount *(why the rule exists)*

| | With approval | Discounted 20% without asking |
|---|---:|---:|
| Client revenue | 150,000 | 120,000 |
| Direct expenses | 40,000 | 40,000 |
| Net profit | 110,000 | 80,000 |
| **Your commission** | **33,000** | **24,000** |

A discount you invent costs you **9,000** — and it teaches the client to negotiate harder next time. If a client needs a lower number, ask us for a scoped-down version instead. Same result for the client, protected profit for both of us.

### Example 8 — A project with no profit

| Line | PKR |
|---|---:|
| Client revenue | 100,000 |
| Direct expenses | 105,000 |
| Net profit | −5,000 |
| Your commission | **0** |

You owe nothing. You are never charged for a loss-making project. But read the expense lines before you accept it — an underpriced project is a pricing failure, not a partner failure, and we will tell you when we think a deal should be walked away from.

## 6. What reduces your commission — and what does not

| Reduces your commission | Does not reduce your commission |
|---|---|
| Documented, approved direct project expenses | Devzard's salaries, overheads and software subscriptions |
| Discounts you offered without written approval | Rework caused by Devzard's own errors |
| Refunds or chargebacks from the client | Penalties we pay because we delivered late |
| Commission adjustments for a client's unpaid invoice | Your own travel, data, phone or marketing costs |
| Tax withheld from your payout where required by law | Sales tax, which is excluded from revenue from the start |

## 7. Payout timeline

```
Client pays Devzard ──► Payment clears ──► Milestone delivered ──► Statement issued ──► Payout within 7 business days
```

- Commission on an invoice is payable only once that invoice is **received and cleared**.
- Multi-milestone projects are paid milestone by milestone.
- Payouts are made by bank transfer (IBFT / Raast) to the account named in your agreement, on the last day of a payout run, or sooner.
- **Minimum payout: PKR 5,000.** Smaller balances carry forward to the next payout.
- A short statement accompanies every payment: project, client revenue, expenses, net profit and your share.

## 8. Verification and disputes

You should never have to take our word for a number.

1. **Ask for the working.** Any project, any time. You get the revenue figure, each expense line, and the resulting split.
2. **Ask for receipts.** Available for every deducted expense without argument.
3. **Raise a query within 15 days** of receiving a statement. Disputed amounts are held back and paid once resolved — the undisputed balance is paid on time regardless.
4. **Escalate.** Unresolved after 15 more days? It goes to the founders, in writing, and the partnership agreement's dispute clause applies.

## 9. Optional: performance bonus

*Not part of the base program. Switch it on if you want to reward your best partners — or delete this section entirely.*

An optional add-on that pays over and above the standard 30%, on verified closed revenue:

| Tier | Closed revenue in a rolling 12 months | Base | Bonus |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Tier 1 | Up to PKR 1,000,000 | 30% | — |
| Tier 2 | PKR 1,000,001 – 3,000,000 | 30% | +3% on net profit above tier 1 |
| Tier 3 | PKR 3,000,001 – 7,500,000 | 30% | +5% on net profit above tier 1 |
| Tier 4 | Above PKR 7,500,000 | 30% | +7% on net profit above tier 1 |

The bonus is applied to the **partner share only** and is paid at the end of each rolling year. Keep it, or ignore it — the 70/30 split works exactly the same without it.
